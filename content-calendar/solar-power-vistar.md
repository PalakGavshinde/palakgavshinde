# Solar Power Vistar Cooperative Society

## Role
Social Media & Admin Head

## Project Overview

Managed social media content and marketing activities for Solar Power Vistar Cooperative Society, with a focus on solar energy awareness, brand communication, audience engagement, and lead generation.

## Key Responsibilities

- Social media content planning
- Instagram content creation
- Reel concepts and scripts
- Reel cover design
- Campaign planning
- Festival and awareness content
- Brand communication
- Social media analytics
- Lead generation initiatives

## Content Strategy

Created educational, awareness-based, promotional, and festival-related content to communicate the benefits of solar energy in a simple and engaging way.

## Campaign Work

Worked on campaigns and creative concepts around:

- Rooftop Solar
- Solar savings
- Net metering awareness
- Solar energy education
- Lead generation
- Local marketing activations
- Festival campaigns

## Creative Work

Developed social media posts, reels, carousel concepts, captions, reel covers, campaign creatives, and promotional content aligned with the brand identity.

## Performance

One Instagram Reel crossed 30,000+ views with significant support from paid advertising.

Performance was evaluated separately for organic and paid reach to understand the actual impact of the content and campaign.
